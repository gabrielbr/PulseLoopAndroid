# Audit: six upstream ports (v2.16.0)

Date: 2026-11-10 · Ports of foureight84/PulseLoopAndroid #125, #127, #129, #130, #133 and #134
(with `3f39c09`) · Auditor: Claude, by reading the code. There is no device in this session, so
there are no screenshots. Each port was rebuilt in this fork's design, not merged as-is.

## Scope (every changed surface)

1. **Ring status (#125).** A new "Reconnecting…" label in the header pill and on the device hero
   card. While reconnecting, the Wearable settings screen shows a Disconnect button (Outlined
   PulseButton).
2. **Coach (#127).** A one-line hint under Web Search for Gemini.
3. **Estimated SpO₂ (#129).**
   - Vitals card: an ESTIMATED chip and a 12sp `warning` footnote, matching the derived-stress
     treatment.
   - Today tile: a one-line "Estimated from heart rate".
   - Vital detail: a disclaimer card and an "Estimated" chip on each estimated row.
4. **Scheduled SpO₂ (#134).**
   - Measurement settings: the heart-rate slider goes to 120 min on YCBT; "Measure SpO₂ from the
     app" with its explanation and 1–3 h slider.
   - "Hide estimated SpO₂" now shows on every supported ring and is **off by default**. This is a
     divergence from upstream, which hid estimates by default; our rule is that a derived metric
     is shown and labelled.
   - The Health Connect respiratory-rate row is disabled on YCBT, with its reason shown.
   - Charts join readings 2–3 h apart.
5. **Sleep (#130).**
   - The edit dialog now trims or extends, and names the limit a pick ran into.
   - Added minutes appear as a hatched "Added by you" band in `textMuted`, with a legend row, a
     line on the Records card, and a mention in the spoken summary.
   - The Records card also shows on single-record nights.
6. **Coach history (#133).**
   - A History button in the coach header, a 48dp target between Usage and New chat.
   - A new sheet: rows with Chevron, swipe to delete plus an accessibility Delete action, "Delete
     all", and two confirm dialogs.
