Date: 2026-11-04 · PR: see below · Auditor: Claude (four parallel read-only passes, one per
journey, each finding checked against the code before it was fixed)

## Scope

Every screen and flow in the app, grouped by journey:

- **Today and chrome:** header, tab bar and rail, Today, the Actions sheet, My Health, Readiness,
  Activity score, What changed, Breathe.
- **Vitals, Sleep, Activity:** Vitals, vital detail, measure, Sleep, hypnogram, Activity, Record,
  live workout, workout summary, log past activity, journal.
- **Nutrition, programs, coach:** Nutrition, meal log (typing, photo, barcode), Drinks, Meds,
  Body, Cycle, Postpartum with the EPDS check-in, Lab reports, Coach and its cards.
- **First run, ring, Settings:** onboarding, pairing, Ring & sync, every Settings group, Accuracy
  check, Health Connect source, Language, the update sheet, widget config.

Known divergences were not reported: D-2 wipe, D-3 dither, D-4 dark only, D-5 tab titles, D-6
48dp rows, D-9 shared hues, P3-2 button pill shape.

**Every skill was read from its vendored copy** (`.claude/skills/*`), so none had to be applied by
hand. Impeccable is scored against `reference/audit.native.md` and `android.md`.

### Findings and what happened

**P0, fixed**
- **The coach's proposed edits and deletes never reached the user.** `pendingActions` was dropped
  in `CoachViewModel`, and `CoachActionCardView` was never shown. Now the confirm card sits under
  the reply, and the change runs only on Confirm (`confirmAction` / `cancelAction`).

**P1, fixed**
- **Raw exception text in the coach, meal analysis and lab reading.** These now show plain
  states, and the exception goes to logcat.
- **Raw tool JSON under every coach reply.** Removed. "Used N tools" is translated, and the trace
  row is a 48dp button.
- **EPDS check-in:**
  - answers had no radio semantics and were about 40dp tall;
  - Back threw the result away;
  - the SAMU/CVV rows were about 32dp tall.
- **Lab reports showed "In range" in mint.** Now `success`.
- **Extra filled mint buttons on tabs:** Vitals Measure, Nutrition "Log this", the HC source cards
  and the coach confirm. All are tonal now.
- **Drinks More/Undo/+ drink were about 24dp targets.** Now 48dp with a button role.
- **Deleting a period took one tap with no confirm.** It now asks first.
- **The derived-stress label was cut to about 24 characters,** which broke the issue #67 rule. It
  is now its own wrapping line.
- **Sleep stage labels, workout labels and many other strings were untranslated.** All wrapped,
  with pt-BR entries.
- **11sp text, through Material's `labelSmall`.** Fixed for the whole app in `Theme.kt`.
- **The Journal day switch was about a 28dp target.** Now 48dp.
- **Widget config had no system-bar insets on targetSdk 35.** It now uses `safeDrawingPadding`.
- **Pairing showed "GATT connect failed: 133".** It shows a plain state now; the raw text stays in
  diagnostics.
- **The Accuracy "Details" link was about a 16dp target.** Now a PulseButton.

**P2, fixed**
- **My Health's load failure was a dead end.** It now offers Try again.
- **Mint used for status:**
  - fixed in What changed, Log sheet "Logged", Drinks (dry days, streak, AUDIT-C), Meds "Taken",
    My Health trend bars, the Lab chart, the Record timer, untracked Sleep bars, the route line
    and the Postpartum chip;
  - tab and selected-chip mint is allowed, since the mint token itself lists the active tab pill.
- **Glow shadows** in Today tiles, VitalCard and the onboarding logo: removed.
- **Bouncy springs** in onboarding and pairing are now `Motion.EMPHASIS_MS` with `Motion.ease`.
  `STANDARD_MS` was added too.
- **The battery chip was always green.** It is tinted by level now.
- **Score centre text was 48sp.** Now 40.
- **The Readiness link** is now a PulseButton.
- **"Start check-in"** is tonal, so the crisis buttons lead.

**P2, carried (next audit)**
- Pairing: the dots overflow with more than 8 models, and the pager's fixed 300dp clips at large
  font scale.
- Record doesn't scroll in landscape.
- Coach photo decode runs on the main thread, and attach / new chat share one icon.
- Dates and times: several are forced to 24 hours or English month names. Use
  `DateFormat.is24HourFormat` and localised patterns.
- Nutrition with no goal shows "of 2000" as if the user had set it.
- Onboarding:
  - the finale promises a baseline at days 3–7, but it is 14 nights;
  - its goal save skips `GoalSync`;
  - "Skip" is about 43dp tall.
- "Unpair & Reset" sits on Backup & data. Unpairing belongs on Ring & sync only.
- Settings sub-screens use `TopAppBar`; the hub and Pairing have no bar.
- Mint is still decorative on the recognised-ring stripe and on Settings icons.
- A Lab review is thrown away on Back or Discard without asking.
- CVV in Drinks Support doesn't dial.
- The vital-detail explainer for derived stress, and its disclaimer placement.
- Toasts in Sleep should be Snackbars.
- Workout map: no spoken summary, and its aspect ratio is distorted.

**P3, carried:**
- off-scale spacing;
- hand-rolled eyebrows and top bars on the remaining screens;
- 18/26dp icons;
- `Color.White` in the chrome;
- clickables without a role;
- title case in a few labels ("Forget Ring", "Add Widget");
- dead code: `HeroInsightCard`, `UnusualSignalsCard`, `SimpleLineChart`, `MetricWithSparkline`,
  `CoachResponseView`, `CoachChartView`;
- `tr(" · %.2f %s")` used as a format key;
- AGENTS.md says the readiness ring wears the brand colour, but Readiness and Activity score
  colour it by band. The code's bands are the more useful reading, so the rule text should
  change.
