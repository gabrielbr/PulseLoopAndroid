# Audit: Bedtime tracking without the sleep schedule (v2.15.1)

Date: 2026-11-10 · Port of upstream foureight84/PulseLoopAndroid#128 (issue #126) · Auditor:
Claude, by reading the code. There is no device in this session, so there are no screenshots.

## Scope

- **Settings › Ring & sync, the sleep trimming card.**
  - Before: "Track Bedtime / DND mode" was nested under "Sleep quiet hours" and hidden while the
    schedule was off.
  - Now: one card, "Sleep trimming", with one warning line and two independent switches,
    Bedtime/DND first and the schedule second. The From/Until buttons show only while the schedule
    is on.
- **Import:** Bedtime-only trims a record to the Bedtime/DND windows it overlaps. A night with no
  Bedtime is kept whole.
- **Migration, one time:** a Bedtime flag left on under a switched-off schedule meant "no trimming",
  so it is cleared rather than starting to trim.
